How it works

The whole month runs in one forum category. Three things happen there, and you can do as much or as little of each as you like.

  1. Post what made you say it

    Open a topic in the forum category and describe one thing. What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A few sentences is plenty.

  2. Vote on the ones you recognize

    Read through what everyone else has posted and vote. Votes are how we see which rough edges a lot of people keep hitting, so this matters as much as posting.

  3. Watch things get picked up

    Contributors read along all month and start on what they can. Not everything can be fixed, but a lot gets fixed, and the rest tells us where to look next.
