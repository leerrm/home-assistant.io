You do not need to be a developer

Home Assistant started out as a project for people who like taking things apart, and a lot of the ways you can give feedback still assume that. This month does not.

You do not need a GitHub account, you do not need to know what anything is called under the hood, and you do not need to write it up neatly. Say what you were trying to do and what got in the way. That is the whole format.

  • You only ever use the Home Assistant app on your phone
  • You live in a home somebody else set up
  • You set it up once and have barely touched it since
  • You have been running it for years and know every corner
