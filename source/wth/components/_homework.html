Before you post

Four small things that make every topic easier to read, vote on, and act on. None of them are hard.

  1. 1

    One thing per topic

    Post each “What the heck?!” separately. There is no limit on how many you can create. Five things in one topic cannot be voted on or picked up one at a time, so they tend to go nowhere together.

  2. 2

    Have a quick look first

    If someone already posted yours, vote on theirs instead. That is more useful than a second topic saying the same thing, and it keeps all the discussion in one place.

  3. 3

    Say what you expected

    The gap between what you expected and what happened is the useful part. It is often the only thing that explains why something feels wrong even when it technically works.

  4. 4

    Keep it to what already exists

    This month is about the Home Assistant you have today. Requests to support a new device, service, or app belong in the Feature Requests category, where they are collected all year round.
