What happens when

One month, start to finish.

  1. 1 November

    The forum category opens and the first topics start going up. A topic posted early has the whole month to collect votes, so there is something to be said for not sitting on yours.

  2. All through November

    Posting, reading, and voting carry on for the full month. Contributors read along and start picking up what they can, so some things are already fixed before the month is out.

  3. 30 November

    The month closes. The category stays readable, and the topics in it stay useful long after.

  4. Afterwards

    We go through everything that came in when planning what to work on next. Several things from past editions took a while to arrive, but arrived because the topic was there.
