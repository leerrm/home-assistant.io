It really does turn into features

Two of the things people now use every day started as somebody being mildly annoyed in a forum topic.

  • WTH can’t we drag & drop in the dashboard?

    Posted in the 2022 edition

    Shipped Drag-and-drop arrived with the sections dashboard, which came out of its experimental phase in 2024.11.

  • Why are all automations in one big list?

    Posted in the 2022 edition

    Shipped Labels, categories, and filtering landed as three new ways to organize in 2024.4.

Neither of those was a feature request. Both were someone saying a thing was odd, and enough other people agreeing.
