What counts as a “What the heck?!”

If you have ever muttered it at a screen, it counts. Here is the kind of thing that fills up the forum every edition.

  • The small annoyance

    Something you do every week that takes three taps more than it should. You have stopped noticing it, right up until you notice it again.

  • The setting you can never find

    You know the option exists. You have changed it before. You still go looking in the wrong place every single time.

  • The thing that is not consistent

    Two parts of Home Assistant that do the same job in two different ways, so you can never quite remember which one you are in.

  • The quiet surprise

    It works. It just does not do what you expected it to do, and you had to find that out the hard way.

  • The small wish

    Not a whole new feature. The one little thing that would make something you already use noticeably nicer.

  • The thing you gave up on

    You tried to set it up once, it did not go well, and you quietly worked around it instead. We would like to know about that too.
